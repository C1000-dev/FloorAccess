Who it is for

Fits

  • You trade futures with a defined process and defined risk.
  • You journal every trade and review the journal.
  • You can explain why a losing trade lost.
  • You share what you know and challenge what you are told.

Does not fit

  • You want signals to copy.
  • You want payout screenshots or a leaderboard.
  • You are selling a course, a channel or a service.
  • You want to be told what to trade.

Members are expected to have real trading knowledge. A less experienced trader who shows sound process and risk control in the interview can still be considered.

What happens inside

Journaling

Every trade is logged with entry, stop, target, result and the reasoning behind it. Losses are posted alongside wins. This is a condition of membership.

Team review

Session preparation, market structure and trade reviews happen in the open. Levels are posted with reasoning, and members are expected to defend them.

Guidance

Experienced members give direct feedback on your process and your journal. Ask for a review of any trade and you will get a specific answer.

Resource library

A short list of selected YouTube videos and written material made inside the server: VWAP, volume profile, price action, Fibonacci and trading psychology. Sorted by topic and added only when it is worth studying.

Markets

Gold (GC, MGC) and Nasdaq (MNQ, NQ, NAS100). Discussion stays on these instruments and on the conditions that move them.

How access works

  1. 1

    Apply

    Complete the application form: your Discord username, how long you have traded futures, which contracts, and how you trade. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 2

    Interview

    A short conversation about your process, your risk rules and your experience. We are checking competence, not credentials.

  3. 3

    Access

    Approved applicants receive an invite to the private Discord and start journaling from their first trade. Invites are not issued any other way.
